  <h2>Submit Your Event</h2>

  <p>
    Want to have your gamedev event listed in our index? Contact us at <a href="mailto:contact@gamedevtreff.com">contact@gamedevtreff.com</a> with the following information:
  </p>

  <ul>
    <li>Name of the event</li>
    <li>City or cities</li>
    <li>Type of event (jam, meetup, conference, workshop, etc.)</li>
    <li>Short description</li>
    <li>Link to the event website</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Guidelines</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>Your link should go to a page with information about the event itself. We do not track when individual events take place.</li>
    <li>Events that have not occurred for 12 months will be delisted.</li>
    <li>Only in-person events are accepted. No online or virtual events.</li>
  </ul>

  <p>
    We review all submissions and add suitable events as soon as possible.
  </p>
