We are hosting frequent events for game developers and game designers in the Munich area. We're a very inclusive group: If you can read this, you are welcome to join us for any of our events at any time. It doesn't matter if you're indie, employed, freelancer or student. Both experts and newcomers are important parts of the group. Join us if you want to meet like-minded people and improve your skills and knowledge in development and design. We explicity welcome people of all colours, nationalities, genders and orientations to come to our meetups. So join us and have a good time.
