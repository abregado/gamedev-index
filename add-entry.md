  <h1>What is GameDevTreff?</h1>

    <p>
    Local game development communities are awesome. They make it easy to meet people with similar goals and interests, to share knowledge, and to keep each other motivated.
    </p>

    <p>
    However, we think they should be easier to find. This website is our attempt to help with that. It is focused on local meetups and events, rather than communities that exist exclusively online.
    </p>

  <br>

  <h1>Add your own Entry</h1>

    <p>
    If you would like to add your own entry to the list, please write to
    <a href="mailto:test@notareallinkyet.com">test@notareallinkyet.com</a>
    </p>

    <p>
    Here is what we will need:
    <ul>
        <li>A name for your local community or event</li>
        <li>A location (just the city, but it can be more than one)</li>
        <li>A single link to a website (or e.g. a Discord invite) where visitors can learn more</li>
        <li>A short description (can be as long as you want, but it will be truncated in the listing)</li>
        <li>Any tags you would like us to use (see the top of the main list for the tags we currently use)</li>
    </ul>
    </p>
    

    <p>
    We maintain this website for fun. But in order for this to stay manageable, it can only be a jumping off point for visitors. We cannot list all possible information for your event, because it would change too often for the website to stay up to date, and to stay useful.    
    </p>

    <p>
    For example, we do not recommend listing exact meeting times or places in your description. Something like "once a month in Frankfurt" is fine, but the rest should be found when following your link.
    </p>
