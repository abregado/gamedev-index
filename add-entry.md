  <h2>Add your own Entry</h2>

    <p>
    If you would like to add your own entry to the list, please write to
    <a href="mailto:test@notareallinkyet.com">test@notareallinkyet.com</a>
    </p>

    <p>
    Here is what we will need:
    <ul>
        <li>A name for your local community or event</li>
        <li>A short description </li>
        <li>One or more cities</li>
        <li>One or more event tags</li>
        <li>A single link where visitors can learn more</li>
    </ul>
    </p>
    
    <h2>Guidelines</h2>

    <p>
    We maintain this website for fun. But in order for this to stay manageable, it can only be a jumping-off point for visitors. We cannot list all possible information for your event, because it would change too often for the website to stay up to date, and to stay useful.    
    </p>

    <h3>Keep it simple</h3>

    <p>
    To make sure your entry stays useful over time (and doesn't have to change constantly), please do not list details such as exact meeting times or places in your description. Something like "once a month in Frankfurt" is fine, but the rest should be found when following your link.
    </p>

    <p>
    Your description can be as long as you want, but it will be truncated in the list view.
    </p>

    <h3>A single link</h3>
    Similarly, we only list a single link for your entry. That makes it less likely for your entry to end up with outdated data, and it makes it easier for visitors to know where to start. Your link should go to a place where they can easily learn what your community or event is all about. It could be your own website, a social media page, or even a Discord server invite.

    <h3>Tags!</h3>
    You can find the tags we currently use at the top of the list. If your event or community fits none of those, feel free to suggest a new one. But getting too specific makes it harder for visitors to search for their interests. So we recommend to also try and find existing tags that fit well enough, even if they aren't a perfect match. If in doubt, just ask.

    <h3>Offline only</h3>
    On this website, we only list events and communities that regularly offer opportunities to meet in-person, at least once per year. They can of course have an online community as well, but they also need to have that local aspect. This is to ensure this website stays useful for that specific purpose, and to promote a type of community that can otherwise be hard to find online.
